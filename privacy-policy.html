Privacy Policy

Social Scheduler
Effective date: 15 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Social Scheduler ("we", "us", "our", or "the Service") handles information when you use the application and connect third-party services such as TikTok.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, Social Scheduler may process:

  • Account identifiers provided by connected platforms, such as TikTok Open ID or similar identifiers.
  • Basic profile information made available through authorized platform APIs.
  • OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens needed to connect your account and perform authorized actions.
  • Content you choose to upload, schedule, publish, or manage through the Service, including titles, descriptions, captions, media files, and scheduling information.
  • Technical and diagnostic information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Service, such as error messages, API responses, and timestamps.

2. How We Use Information

We use information only as necessary to provide and support Social Scheduler, including to:

  • Authenticate and connect your authorized third-party accounts.
  • Upload, schedule, publish, edit, or manage content when you instruct the Service to do so.
  • Maintain connection status and refresh authorization where permitted.
  • Display account or publishing information inside the application.
  • Diagnose errors, enforce security, and improve reliability.
  • Comply with applicable legal and platform requirements.

3. TikTok Data

If you connect TikTok, Social Scheduler may access only the TikTok information and permissions you expressly authorize through TikTok's OAuth process.

Depending on the approved scopes, this may include account identifiers, basic profile information, publishing permissions, and information needed to upload or publish content on your behalf.

Social Scheduler does not ask for or store your TikTok password. Authentication is handled through TikTok's authorization system.

4. OAuth Tokens and Credentials

Access tokens and refresh tokens are security credentials. They are used only to communicate with the connected platform APIs and perform actions you authorize.

You should protect any device or local configuration that stores these credentials. You may revoke access through the relevant third-party platform, including TikTok, which may cause connected features to stop working until you authorize them again.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party platforms only as needed to provide the functionality you request. For example, when you publish a video to TikTok, the content and associated metadata are sent to TikTok through its authorized API.

We may also disclose information if required by law, court order, or a lawful government request, or when reasonably necessary to protect the security, rights, or safety of the Service or others.

6. Third-Party Services

Social Scheduler may connect to services such as TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, WhatsApp, email providers, and other platforms. Each third-party service is governed by its own privacy policy and terms.

We are not responsible for how a third-party platform independently collects, uses, retains, or discloses information after it receives data under its own services and policies.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, maintain security and functionality, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

Authorization tokens may remain stored while an account is connected. When access is revoked or no longer required, related credentials should be removed or invalidated where technically possible.

8. Data Deletion and Revoking Access

You may disconnect Social Scheduler from a third-party platform by revoking its authorization through that platform's account settings.

You may also request deletion of personal information held by Social Scheduler by contacting the operator using the support contact published on the official Social Scheduler or MMCJ website.

Some information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security, fraud-prevention, or record-keeping purposes.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no internet transmission, local storage method, or electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

Social Scheduler is not intended for use by children who are not legally permitted to use the connected third-party services. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children outside the requirements of applicable law and platform policies.

11. International Data Transfers

Connected third-party platforms may process information in countries outside Australia. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

12. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your use of the Service.

You may also withdraw platform authorization at any time through the relevant third-party service, subject to that platform's controls and policies.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

14. Contact

Before publishing: replace this notice with your real support email address or other official contact method so users can submit privacy or deletion requests.

For privacy questions, access requests, or deletion requests, contact the operator of Social Scheduler through the official support contact published on the Social Scheduler or MMCJ website.
